Orbital Defender Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Game data

Orbital Defender does not require an account. Game progress, high scores, unlocked cores, and settings are stored on your device. The core game can be played offline. Ads and ad-supported rewards require an internet connection.

Advertising and analytics

The app uses Google AdMob to display ads and Google Firebase Analytics to understand app use and performance. These services may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, app and ad interactions, crash reports, and performance data. Depending on your choices and the service, some data may be linked to a device and used for advertising measurement or tracking across apps and websites. The App Store privacy label lists the data categories and purposes reported for this app.

The app asks for consent where required for ads and requests iOS App Tracking Transparency permission when applicable. Declining tracking permission does not remove ads or all data processing needed for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, or app operation.

Your choices

You can manage tracking permission in iOS Settings. Where required, the game's Settings page provides an advertising privacy options control. You can play offline without ad requests; ad-supported rewards will then be unavailable. You can remove locally stored game data by deleting the app.

Third-party policies and contact

Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy. For questions about Orbital Defender, use our support page.
